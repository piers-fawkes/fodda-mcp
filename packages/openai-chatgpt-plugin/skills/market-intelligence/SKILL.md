# Fodda Market & Brand Intelligence

Grounded research across retail, consumer goods, technology, sustainability, and culture knowledge graphs.

### Workflow & Attribution Guidelines:
- Combine Fodda's expert-curated graph trends, quantitative statistics, corporate earnings disclosures, and macroeconomic data:
  1. Lead with relevant graph trends and their signal scores (`search_graph`).
  2. Support with curated data points (`search_statistics`), qualitative quotes/insights (`search_insights`), or earnings commentary (`get_company_earnings`).
  3. Validate broader industry dynamics or macroeconomic tailwinds using supplemental context (`get_supplemental_context`).
- Always attribute insights to their specific source graph or named practitioner.
- Digital Twins are AI models grounded in expert knowledge graphs, not live people.
